# QR Bank

Scan a poster's QR code with your phone camera to get the Pokémon's .pk file back.

1. Open the site on your phone (in Safari on iPhone: Share › Add to Home Screen).
2. Allow the camera.
3. Point it at the poster's QR code. Tap Bookmark to keep it in your bank, or Download to save the .pk file.

Your bank lives in this browser only. Clearing browsing data deletes it, so use Export now and then to keep a backup file. After the first visit it also works offline.
